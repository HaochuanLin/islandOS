## D. Evidence quality and confidence

Use these classifications:
- **Verified official record:** directly present in a current official dataset, within its stated coverage.
- **Reported secondary estimate:** reported by a credible publication but not independently traceable to facility-level records.
- **Inferred:** derived from proxies or assumptions; never mix into verified counts.
- **Unknown:** insufficient evidence to classify.

A national electrification rate, installed MW, population without electricity or number of mini-grid projects is not automatically a count of IslandOS-eligible facilities.
