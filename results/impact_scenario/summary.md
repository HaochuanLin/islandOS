# Synthetic impact scenario (30 random weeks, simulated)

Generic off-grid island: 60 kW solar, 40 kW wind, 120 kWh battery, 80 kW diesel, 15 kW RO, 80 m3 tank, about 40 m3 of water demand per day.

After adjusting for water left in the tank, coordinated scheduling used less diesel in 3 of 30 weeks. The diesel change ranged from -0.8% to +6.3% across weeks.

All numbers are simulation results on synthetic inputs. They show how the model behaves, not what a real island would save.
